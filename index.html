        📖 How to Play Bhabhi Thulla

        Bhabhi Thulla, also known as Getaway, is a classic Punjabi shedding card game. The goal is simple: get rid of all your cards. The last player still holding cards is the Bhabhi (the loser).

        ⚡ How to play in 30 seconds

        • Whoever holds the Ace of Spades starts by playing it.
        • On your turn, play a card of the same suit as the card that was led — if you have one.
        • If you don’t have that suit, you may play any card — that is a Thulla. It ends the trick at once, and the player with the highest card of the led suit picks up all the cards played.
        • Play your last card and you escape, safe. The last player left holding cards is the Bhabhi!

        🃏 Setup

        • Standard 52-card deck, no jokers. Ranks from high to low: A K Q J 10 9 8 7 6 5 4 3 2. There are no trump suits.
        • 3 or 4 players. All 52 cards are dealt: with 3 players the hands are 18 / 17 / 17; with 4 players everyone gets 13 cards.
        • Play goes clockwise.

        🎴 The first trick

        • The player holding the Ace of Spades must lead it face-up.
        • Every other player plays one card, clockwise. Players holding a spade must play a spade; players with no spade may play any card.
        • A Thulla does not stop the first trick — everyone still plays.
        • All first-trick cards go to the waste pile, and the Ace of Spades holder leads the second trick.

        🎴 Later tricks

        • The leader plays any card. Each following player must play a card of the led suit if they hold one.
        • If a player holds no card of the led suit, they may play any card — that off-suit card is the Thulla (also called tochoo).
        • A Thulla ends the trick immediately: players after the Thulla player do not play.

        🏆 Winning and losing a trick

        • Clean trick (everyone followed suit): the cards are discarded to the waste pile, and the player who played the highest card of the led suit leads the next trick.
        • Thulla trick: the player who played the highest card of the led suit picks up all the cards played in that trick and leads the next trick.

        🏃 Getting away & the Bhabhi

        • A player who plays their last card is out and safe — they have escaped.
        • House rule (power edge case): if a player’s last card was the highest of the led suit in a clean trick, they would normally hold the power to lead — but they have no cards left. In this game they are out and safe, and the lead passes to the next active player clockwise.
        • Play continues until only one player still holds cards — that player is the Bhabhi.
        • Tiebreak: if all remaining players empty their hands on the same trick, the player who led that trick is the Bhabhi.
        • Play again to settle the score — the scoreboard counts how many times each player has been the Bhabhi.
